## Cosmetic dentistry and a natural-looking smile

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on the harmony of tooth shape, colour and appearance while preserving function. The aim is to choose a solution that suits your wishes, the condition of your teeth and the natural features of your smile.

Before any cosmetic procedure it is important to assess the health of the teeth and gums, the bite and realistic expectations of the result.

## Veneers

Dental veneers can be considered to correct the shape, colour or minor irregularities of the front teeth. Suitability, material and the preparation needed depend on the condition of the teeth and the individual treatment plan.

## Tooth whitening

Whitening can lighten the natural colour of teeth. The health of the teeth and gums needs to be checked before the procedure. Existing crowns, bridges and fillings do not change colour in the same way as natural teeth, which is important to bear in mind when planning a smile.

## Composite cosmetic corrections

Composite materials can be used to correct certain minor irregularities in tooth shape or to restore damaged parts of the tooth surface. The choice of procedure depends on the extent of the change, the bite and the condition of the teeth.

## How do we choose a cosmetic solution?

**1. Examination and conversation.** We get to know your wishes and assess the health of your teeth and gums.

**2. Planning.** We consider the possible procedures, the expected changes and the limitations of each solution.

**3. Treatment and check.** The procedure follows the agreed plan, with a check of appearance and function.

**4. Maintenance.** After the procedure you receive instructions on hygiene, habits and check-ups.
