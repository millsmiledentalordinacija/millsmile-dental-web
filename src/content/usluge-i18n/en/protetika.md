## What is dental prosthetics?

Dental prosthetics makes it possible to replace missing teeth or restore damaged ones, with an emphasis on a natural look, function and comfort. The type of prosthetic work is chosen according to the condition of your teeth, your bite and your individual needs.

## Dental crowns and bridges

**Dental crowns** can restore the shape, appearance and function of a tooth that is considerably damaged or weakened, when such an approach is clinically justified.

**Dental bridges** can replace one or more missing teeth by relying on suitable abutment teeth or, in some cases, on implants. The choice depends on the condition of the remaining teeth and the treatment options.

## A dental laboratory within the practice

Because the dental laboratory is on the same premises as the practice, the shape, shade and other details of the prosthetic work can be coordinated with you directly. This cooperation allows aesthetic and functional expectations to be agreed more precisely, with each piece of work planned individually.

## How is prosthetic work made?

**1. Examination and planning.** The condition of the teeth and bite is assessed, and your wishes and the treatment options are discussed.

**2. Preparation and taking an impression or digital scan.** The procedure depends on the chosen prosthetic solution and the clinical findings.

**3. Fabrication and try-in.** The shape, shade, fit and function of the work are checked and adjustments are made where needed.

**4. Fitting and maintenance.** You receive instructions on hygiene and check-ups, adapted to the type of prosthetic work.
