## What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics deals with irregular tooth position and the relationship between the jaws, with the aim of improving bite function, making hygiene easier to maintain and achieving a harmonious smile.

## When to consider an orthodontic examination?

An examination can be useful if you notice crowding or gaps between teeth, bite irregularities or changes in tooth position. Suitability and the right time to start treatment are determined individually.

## How is orthodontic treatment planned?

**1. Examination and diagnostics.** Tooth position, bite and oral health are assessed, along with the need for further diagnostics.

**2. Treatment plan.** The available treatment options and expected duration are considered, depending on the findings.

**3. Monitoring treatment.** While the teeth are being moved, check-ups and following instructions are important.

**4. Maintaining results.** After active treatment, retention may be needed to preserve the tooth position achieved.

The type of orthodontic appliance and the duration of treatment depend on the complexity of the case and the individual plan.
