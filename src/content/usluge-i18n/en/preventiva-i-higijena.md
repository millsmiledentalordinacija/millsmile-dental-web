## Why are preventive check-ups important?

Regular dental check-ups help detect tooth decay, gum disease and other changes in the mouth in good time. Even when you have no pain, an examination can reveal changes that are not yet causing problems.

## Professional oral hygiene

Professional removal of tartar and deposits complements daily brushing and cleaning between the teeth. The procedure and its frequency are adapted to the condition of your teeth and gums.

## Daily care of teeth and gums

Correct brushing, cleaning between the teeth and regular check-ups are important for preserving oral health. At your examination you can get individual recommendations on cleaning technique and suitable aids.

## What does a preventive visit look like?

**1. Examination.** The condition of the teeth, gums and mouth is assessed.

**2. Recommendation.** Based on the findings, the hygiene procedures needed and further check-ups are agreed.

**3. Maintenance instructions.** You receive advice on daily hygiene adapted to your needs.
